Privacy Policy

Digest: News & Learn Offline · Last updated 8 November 2026 (version 2.0, ads added to the Android app)

Short version: Digest itself does not collect, sell or share any personal information, and there are no accounts. The Android app on Google Play shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising ID for them, as explained below. The website version has no ads.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in Digest is stored only on your own phone, in the app's local storage:

  • Articles downloaded for offline reading, and notes you write yourself.
  • Saved articles, reading progress, highlights and quiz answers.
  • Your settings (language, theme, text size, region, daily goal) and reading stats (streaks, badges, reading time).

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Notes & backup → Reset library, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Internet use

When you are online, Digest downloads the app and its articles from the website it is hosted on (GitHub Pages). Like any website, the hosting provider may record standard technical information, such as your IP address, in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement. Digest itself sends no information about you or your reading.

Articles contain links to their original sources. If you open one, you leave Digest and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Your region

To show slightly more local news, Digest guesses your country from your phone's time zone and language setting. This happens entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere. Digest does not use GPS or location permission.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Digest does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Sharing

When you share an article, quote card or progress image, your phone's share menu sends it only to the app you choose.

Ads in the Android app

To keep Digest free, the Google Play version shows light ads from Google AdMob: one small banner at the end of an article, and an optional short video you can choose to watch for 24 hours without ads. There are no pop-up ads. Ads are not shown during your first days of use, during quizzes or read-aloud, or when you are offline.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), and basic information about the ads you see and tap. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Digest never sends your reading, notes, highlights or quiz answers to Google or anyone else.

Children

Digest is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
